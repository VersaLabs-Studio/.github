# VersaLabs Studio

VersaLabs Studio makes ready-to-use business software and builds custom systems for companies: ERP, online stores,
websites and AI tools. Based in Addis Ababa, working with clients in Ethiopia and abroad.

[Explore Our Products](https://www.versalabs-studio.com/products) · [Start a Project](https://www.versalabs-studio.com/contact) · [versalabs-studio.com](https://www.versalabs-studio.com)

## Our Products

| Product | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Obsidian ERP](https://www.versalabs-studio.com/products/obsidian-erp) | Sales, buying, stock, production and accounts in one system. One-click steps cut the busywork, so nothing falls through the cracks. |
| [SafeFrame](https://www.versalabs-studio.com/products/safeframe) | Checks videos for inappropriate content right on the phone or laptop. Nothing is uploaded, so what a child watches stays private. |
| [Versa FX](https://www.versalabs-studio.com/products/versa-fx) | The teller counter, rates, branch cash, compliance and reports for a licensed forex bureau, in one place. In development, with a live demo on sample data. |
